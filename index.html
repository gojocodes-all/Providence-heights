About the School

A school shaped by learning, discipline and the energy of Lagos.

Providence Heights Secondary School is part of the Providence Schools story in Lagos. The school serves students in the Fagba/Iju axis with a focus on academic preparation, values, leadership and personal growth.

The school environment reflects the strength of Lagos Mainland: confidence, resilience, communication, community and ambition.
